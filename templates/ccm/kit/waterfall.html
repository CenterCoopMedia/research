Source and limitations

What the squares mean

The labels use the input values; the drawing uses whole squares. Categories must be mutually exclusive and cover the intended population. A person, dollar, or other unit cannot belong to two groups at once.

How the reveal works

Scroll position controls the cascade. Each group has a filling interval followed by a reading interval: the squares stop at the color boundary while its label remains visible. The final group gets more scrolling room. There is no forced scrolling, snapping, or autoplay. Reverse scrolling reverses the reveal. “Less motion” places new squares directly in the grid.

Small groups first. The full scale last.No tracking. No external libraries. Your data stays in this browser.

Keep the effect. Change the story.

Give each group a value, a color, and a line of narration. The grid, proportions, boundary labels, and scrolling sequence rebuild together. Use non-overlapping groups; the population total is their sum.
